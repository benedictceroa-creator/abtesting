✂️ Tailoring Career Pathway

⚡ Electrical & Trade Career Pathway

Electrical and appliance repair graduates are in strong and growing demand. With additional licensing, experienced electricians can become independent contractors earning significantly above average. Home Appliance Repair graduates can become service technicians very quickly.

Electrician Path
✓
Now
Complete Electrical & Electronics Course
Covers household wiring, circuits, basic appliance repair and safety fundamentals.
1
0–6 months
Electrical Helper / Apprentice
₹8,000 – ₹11,000/month
Work alongside licensed electricians on domestic and commercial wiring projects. Learn on site.
⏱ Fresher-eligible
2
1–3 years
Electrician (Licensed)
₹15,000 – ₹25,000/month
Obtain wireman licence from State Electricity Board. Handle independent wiring jobs and panel installations.
★
3–5 years
Electrical Contractor
₹25,000 – ₹60,000+/month
Bid for commercial contracts, manage a small crew, handle apartment and office wiring projects.

✓

1
0–6 months

⏱ Fresher-eligible
2

₹30,000 – ₹70,000+/month

2

★
3–5 years
Lab Supervisor / Senior Technologist
₹25,000 – ₹40,000/month
Manage lab operations, train junior staff, liaise with doctors on results.

🏥 Nursing Assistant Career Pathway

Nursing Assistants (10th Pass eligible) enter the healthcare system at an entry level and are in steady demand across government and private hospitals. This pathway has clear progression and is one of the most respected vocational routes available.

Healthcare Employment Path
✓
Now
Complete Nursing Assistant Course
10th Pass eligible. Covers patient care, vitals monitoring, nursing procedures and drug administration.
1
0–6 months
Healthcare / Nursing Assistant
₹10,000 – ₹14,000/month
Ward assistance, vitals monitoring, patient care support at govt or private hospitals. Day and night shifts.
⏱ Fresher-eligible · 10th Pass
2
1–3 years
Experienced Care Assistant
₹15,000 – ₹22,000/month
Handle more complex care responsibilities, assist in procedures, become a trusted team member.
3
3–5 years
Senior Care Assistant / Team Leader
₹22,000 – ₹32,000/month
Train new assistants, co-ordinate ward schedules, work in specialised wards (ICU, OT).
★
5+ years (with further study)
Registered Nurse (GNM / B.Sc Nursing)
₹30,000 – ₹55,000/month
Completing a GNM or B.Sc Nursing programme significantly increases responsibilities and salary. Government hospital roles are available.
Typical salary progression
Fresher assistant
₹10–14k
After 2–3 years
₹22–32k
With GNM / B.Sc Nursing
₹30–55k

🎒 Pre-Primary Teaching Career Pathway

Pre-primary teaching is one of the most family-friendly career options — school hours, no evening work, and genuine job satisfaction. Graduates work in playschools, Anganwadis and private schools. Further qualifications (B.Ed, D.El.Ed) open government school opportunities.

Teaching Path
✓
Now
Complete Pre-Primary Teachers Training
10-month course. Covers child development, classroom management, Montessori and activity-based teaching.
1
0–6 months
Pre-Primary Teacher (Playschool / Anganwadi)
₹10,000 – ₹15,000/month
Teach Nursery or LKG. School hours only (8am–1:30pm). Government Anganwadi roles also available.
⏱ Fresher-eligible · School hours
2
1–3 years
Class Teacher (UKG / Class 1)
₹15,000 – ₹22,000/month
Take on higher classes, coordinate with parents, mentor new teachers. Build your reputation within the school.
★
3–5 years (with D.El.Ed / B.Ed)
Government School Teacher
₹28,000 – ₹50,000+/month
D.El.Ed or B.Ed qualification makes you eligible for government primary school teacher posts through TET/DSC examinations. Significant pay and job security.
Typical salary progression
Fresher playschool
₹10–15k
After 2 years
₹15–22k
Govt school (B.Ed/D.El.Ed)
₹28–50k+
