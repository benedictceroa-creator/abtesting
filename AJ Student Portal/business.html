Tailoring & Embroidery Business
From a single sewing machine in your home, you can build a steady income doing what you already know. Thousands of women in Secunderabad and Hyderabad have built successful tailoring units starting with zero investment in premises — working from home, then growing.
Work from home Low startup cost School uniform orders Bridal blouses Maggam embroidery
🛒
What you can offer
  • Blouse stitching (everyday and bridal)
  • Churidar and salwar stitching
  • School uniform stitching (great bulk orders)
  • Alteration and repair work
  • Frock and children's clothing
  • Maggam work and stone embellishment
  • Saree fall and pico work
  • Kurti and Western garment stitching
📣
How to get your first customers
  • Tell 10 neighbours and family members you are taking orders — your first customers are already around you.
  • Post a WhatsApp status with a photo of your work and your rates. Do this every 2–3 days.
  • Contact nearby schools in July–August — uniform orders can be 50–200 pieces from one school.
  • Partner with a fabric shop — they refer customers to you and you refer fabric buyers to them.
  • Post work photos on a Facebook page and Instagram. Even 200 followers in your local area can bring steady orders.
  • Ask every satisfied customer to send you one referral. Word of mouth is your strongest marketing.

Setting Up Your Business

Legal, banking and marketing essentials

These steps apply to every type of self-employment business. Most are free and take less than a day to complete. Doing them early protects you and builds credibility with clients.

📜Udyam Registration (Free MSME Certificate)
Register your business free at udyamregistration.gov.in — takes 15 minutes, requires Aadhaar and PAN only.
You receive a Udyam Registration Certificate — this is your official MSME certificate (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprise).
Benefits: easier bank loans, priority for government tenders, lower interest rates on Mudra loans.
Choose NIC code: 14100 (Tailoring), 96020 (Beautician), 43210 (Electrical), 18120 (DTP/Printing).
🏦Open a Business Bank Account
Visit any nationalised bank with your Udyam certificate, Aadhaar, PAN, and a passport photo.
Ask for a "Jan Dhan for Business" or "Current Account Basic" — many banks offer zero-balance business accounts.
Keeping business money separate from personal money makes tax filing easier and looks more professional to clients.
Share your UPI ID or QR code with customers — digital payments increase trust and simplify records.
📊GST Registration — When You Need It
You do NOT need GST registration until your annual income exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for some states and services).
When starting out, you are exempt. Do not spend money on CA fees for GST registration until you are close to this threshold.
Once registered, you can charge GST and claim input tax credit on your business purchases.
Register free at gstin.gov.in when the time comes — or ask the nearest Jan Seva Kendra for help.
💳Mudra Loan — Funding Your Business
Shishu: up to ₹50,000 — for buying a sewing machine, tool kit, or beauty kit. No collateral.
Kishore: ₹50,000–₹5 lakh — for a small premises or equipment upgrade.
Tarun: ₹5–10 lakh — for establishing a proper shop or service centre.
Apply at any government bank with your Udyam certificate, Aadhaar, a simple business plan, and 6 months of bank statements.

Keeping simple business records

You do not need accounting software to start. A simple notebook or WhatsApp note is enough — but you must keep records. Here is what to track:

  • Income: Date | Customer name | Service | Amount received. Review weekly.
  • Expenses: Date | What you bought | Amount. Keep every receipt.
  • Pending payments: List of customers who have not yet paid and the amount owed.
  • Monthly review: Income minus expenses = your profit. Compare month to month.
Rule: Never mix personal money with business money. Even if you are working from home, transfer a fixed "salary" to your personal account each month. This helps you understand whether your business is truly profitable.
