Self Introduction

How to Introduce Yourself

A confident self-introduction sets the tone for the entire interview. Select your course to get a script tailored to your background — then practise it out loud until it feels natural.

Tips for a strong self-introduction
  • 1
    Greet first — "Good morning Sir/Madam" before you begin. It shows confidence and respect.
  • 2
    Speak slowly and clearly. Do not rush. It is better to speak simple English confidently than to use difficult words incorrectly.
  • 3
    Mention your Asha Jyothi certificate specifically — it is recognised by the Intermediate Board of Telangana and carries weight with employers.
  • 4
    Say one strength and one thing you are eager to learn. Employers like candidates who are honest and motivated.
  • 5
    End with "Thank you" and a smile. A polite close is always remembered.
  • 6
    Practise in front of a mirror at least 5 times before your interview day. Confidence comes from preparation.

Salary Questions

How to answer salary questions

Salary questions are one of the hardest parts of an interview. Here is how to handle them confidently.

If asked: "What salary do you expect?"
Do your research first. Check the salary range in your field. Then say:

"Sir/Madam, I am a fresher and I am willing to start at an amount that is fair for my role. I am focused on learning and growing. I am open to discussing whatever is standard for this position."
If the salary offered is lower than expected
Be polite but direct:

"Thank you for the offer. I was expecting something closer to ₹[amount] based on the market for this role. Is there any flexibility? I am very interested in joining your team."
Always confirm before joining
Before accepting any job offer, confirm in writing: gross salary, any deductions (PF, ESI), working hours, leave entitlement, and job location. If it is not in writing, ask politely:

"Could you please share the offer letter so I can confirm the details?"

Before & During Your Interview

Interview Tips

Small things make a big difference. Being prepared, well-dressed and respectful will set you apart from other candidates.

👔
What to wear
  • Wear clean, ironed clothes — neat and simple is always correct
  • Salwar kameez or saree is professional and appropriate for women in most roles
  • Formal shirt and trousers for men — avoid t-shirts and jeans
  • Clean, polished footwear — avoid slippers
  • Minimal jewellery — one or two simple pieces
  • Hair neatly tied or combed — avoid heavy perfume
🕐
Before the interview
  • Arrive 10–15 minutes early — never arrive late
  • Know the location the day before — do a trial journey if needed
  • Research the company — know what they do and how many people they employ
  • Read your resume the night before — be ready to explain everything on it
  • Get a good night's sleep — rest improves confidence and clarity
  • Eat a light meal before — not too heavy, not empty
🤝
During the interview
  • Greet the interviewer with "Good morning / afternoon Sir / Madam"
  • Sit up straight — good posture shows confidence
  • Maintain gentle eye contact — don't stare, don't look away constantly
  • Speak clearly and at a normal pace — do not rush
  • If you don't understand a question, say "Could you please repeat that?"
  • Do not speak negatively about previous employers or your college
  • Switch off or silence your phone before entering
❓
Questions to ask the employer
  • "What would a typical working day look like in this role?"
  • "What opportunities are there to learn and grow in this position?"
  • "Could you tell me about the team I would be working with?"
  • "What are the working hours and leave policies?"
  • "When can I expect to hear back from you about next steps?"

📝 Questions to ask yourself before accepting a job
What exactly is the salary, and what will be deducted?
Confirm gross salary, and ask if PF and ESI will be deducted. These are not deductions to fear — they protect you. But know the exact take-home amount before you agree.
Where exactly will I be working?
Confirm the work location (address), not just the area. Check how long it takes to travel and whether transport is provided.
What are the working hours and weekly off?
Ask clearly: "What time does work start and finish? How many days a week? Which day is my weekly off?"
Will I receive a written offer letter?
Any genuine employer will provide an offer letter. If they refuse or say "we don't do that here," treat it as a warning sign. Do not resign from another job or move to a new city without a written offer.
Is there a probation period and what are the conditions?
Many jobs have a 3–6 month probation. During this time, your employment can end with shorter notice. Ask what the conditions are and whether salary is lower during probation.
