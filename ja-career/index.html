What to do next

  1. Read the guides for your three areas. They list the exams, colleges, and a plan for each year of school.
  2. Talk to someone who does this work. Ask a relative, teacher, or family friend what an ordinary day in their job is really like.
  3. Share this report with your class teacher and parents. They can help you choose your Class 11 subjects with your results in mind.
  4. Take the questionnaire again next year. Interests change as you grow, and that's normal.

Ask a question

Ask about any career, entrance exam, or college in India. Answers come from an AI assistant, so check important details with your teacher.
