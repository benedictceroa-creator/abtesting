About Asha Garment Factory

Manufacturing Excellence from India to the World Since 2011

Asha Garment Factory (AGF) is an ethical clothing manufacturer based in Hyderabad, India. Since 2011, we've produced premium garments for brands in the USA, UK, Switzerland, Qatar and beyond — with an unwavering commitment to fair wages, safe working conditions and minimal environmental impact.

We offer complete garment production management — from initial design and fabric sourcing to finished product and global delivery. And every garment we produce supports a greater purpose: restoring dignity to orphaned children and empowering single mothers across India.

  • Complete end-to-end garment production management
  • Ethical manufacturing with fair wages and safe working conditions
  • Fabric waste minimisation and sustainability practices
  • Superior stitching, colour fastness, durability and premium finish
  • Exporting to USA, UK, Europe and Middle East since 2011

We manufacture women's wear (dresses, tops, blouses), men's wear (shirts, t-shirts, polos, trousers), kids wear (tops, bottoms, sets, school uniforms), and lifestyle and activewear. All categories are available as OEM or private label production.

Yes — we offer complete private label and OEM manufacturing. This includes custom branding, woven or printed labels, hangtags, and packaging. We work from your designs, tech packs, mood boards, or reference samples.
