About John's Academy

Preparing Today's Learners for Tomorrow's World

At our school, we believe education should go beyond textbooks and examinations. Our vision is to nurture future-ready individuals who are confident, curious, adaptable and equipped to navigate an ever-changing world.

Through a balanced blend of academics, experiential learning, practical exploration and creative expression, we encourage students to think critically, communicate effectively and approach challenges with confidence.

We place equal importance on sports, fitness and extracurricular activities — recognising that physical well-being, discipline, teamwork and resilience are essential to a child's overall development.

Our Curriculum

SSC Education — Nursery to Grade 10

A structured, nurturing journey from your child's very first day of school all the way through the SSC Board examinations.

🌱
Stage 1
Nursery

Play & Discover

Play-based learning through phonics, counting, colours and creative exploration — building curiosity and a love for school.

▶
🔤
Stage 2
UKG & Grade 1–2

Read, Write & Count

Foundational literacy and numeracy in Telugu and English — building confident readers and early mathematicians.

▶
📖
Stage 3
Grade 3–5

Explore & Question

Subject specialisation begins — Science, Social Studies, Maths and Languages taught through projects and activity-based methods.

▶
🔬
Stage 4
Grade 6–8

Analyse & Create

Deeper academic rigour with lab sessions, science fairs, group projects and critical thinking activities across all subjects.

▶
🎓
Stage 5
Grade 9–10

Excel & Graduate

Intensive SSC Board preparation with focused coaching, mock exams and mentorship — setting students up for confident results.
